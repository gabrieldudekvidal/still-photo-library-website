What is this app?

This app is a simple local photo library designed to help you enjoy and organize your photos without ads or distractions.

Does it upload my photos?

No. Your photos stay locally on your device.

Can I edit my photos?

No. The focus is on viewing and appreciating your photos, not editing them.

What formats are supported?

Still supports:

Standard Web: JPEG (.jpg), PNG (.png), WebP (.webp)

Apple & Professional: HEIC (.heic), TIFF (.tiff)

RAW Camera: Sony (.arw), Adobe DNG (.dng), Nikon (.nef)

How about future updates?

We are constantly working to improve support for additional formats, performance, and enhanced interface.
