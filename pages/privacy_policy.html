Still Photo Library Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 3, 2026

Thank you for using Still Photo Library. Still Photo Library is a simple, local-first photo library for macOS. Your privacy is a core part of the app's design. The app does not require an account, does not upload your photos to the cloud, and does not collect your personal information.

Information We Collect

Still Photo Library does not collect any personal information.

The app does not require you to:

  • Create an account
  • Sign in
  • Provide your name, email address, or any other personal information

Your Photos

Your photos remain on your Mac at all times. Still Photo Library only accesses the folders that you choose. The app uses this access solely to display and organize your local photo library.

Your photos are never:

  • Uploaded to the cloud
  • Sent to the developer
  • Shared with third parties
  • Used for advertising, analytics, or machine learning

Local Data

The app may store local preferences, such as your selected photo library folder and application settings. This information is stored only on your Mac and is never transmitted elsewhere.

Internet Connection

Still Photo Library is designed to work offline. An internet connection is not required to browse and manage your photo library.

Analytics and Tracking

Still Photo Library does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Tracking technologies
  • User profiling

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with third-party services that collect or process your personal information.

Children's Privacy

Still Photo Library does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date at the top of this document.
